## Adding more

Kenney's full catalogue is at https://kenney.nl/assets and is almost entirely CC0. Other
reliable CC0 sources: [OpenGameArt (CC0 filter)](https://opengameart.org),
[itch.io CC0 assets](https://itch.io/game-assets/free).

**Before adding a pack**, check its licence. CC0 and "public domain" are safe to vendor.
CC-BY needs attribution here. CC-BY-SA and GPL impose conditions on the whole project —
do not add those without deciding deliberately.

After adding anything, update `assets/INDEX.md` so the AI knows it exists. An index that
has drifted from reality causes confident references to files that are not there, which
show up as invisible sprites and silent audio.
