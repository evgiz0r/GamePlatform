# drive by

A rail shooter seen from a car window. The player's sketch: a car, a view out of the
window onto the street's buildings, and creatures on the buildings to hit as they pass.
Then: bananas instead of bullets, a road that bends, and a head that moves.

## What is it called?

drive by

## Who are you?

Someone in a car with a bag of bananas, looking out of the side window and a little
forward, the way you actually look out of a car. The road bends left and right and the
car follows it, at whatever speed it is doing; your head drifts a little as you look. No
extra controls on the phone, and no crosshair cluttering the view.
The game opens with a three-second shot from across the street that swoops into the
driver's seat; then the car pulls away and it begins.

## What do you do?

Tap and you lob a banana at that spot. It arcs through the air and explodes where it
lands; every goblin in the blast is done for, so a good throw takes out two or three at
once. (Keys and pad: arrows move an invisible aim point, A throws. Taps aim by
themselves.) One kill right after another chains a combo: 10, then 20, then 30.

## What is trying to stop you?

The goblins, perched on windowsills and roofs, sliding past. Any goblin that leaves the
window alive costs a life. Three lives. The car speeds up over time, slows down for
traffic now and then, and the goblins come more often. People stroll the sidewalk and
they are not targets: a banana going off next to one is -5 and a fright, nothing worse.

## How do you win?

Highest score. It is an endless drive.

## What should it look like?

A street at night: shops and towers, trees and street lights between them, people
walking, a far skyline and clouds drifting more slowly than the street (so it feels
deep). The car interior frames the view: pillars, roof, door sill, and the next banana in
your hand in the bottom right corner.

## Core loop

Watch the street roll by, spot the goblin, lob a banana before it is gone. Faster and faster.
