# drive by

A rail shooter seen from a car window. The player's sketch: a car, a view out of the
window onto the street's buildings, and creatures on the buildings to shoot as they pass.

## What is it called?

drive by

## Who are you?

Someone in a car with a gun, looking out of the side window. The view never moves; the
street does, at whatever speed the car is doing. No extra controls on the phone.

## What do you do?

Tap a goblin and you shoot it. That is the whole control. (Keys and pad: arrows move a
crosshair, A fires. Taps aim by themselves.) Shooting one goblin right after another
chains a combo: 10, then 20, then 30.

## What is trying to stop you?

The goblins, perched on windowsills and roofs, sliding past. Any goblin that leaves the
window alive costs a life. Three lives. The car speeds up over time, slows down for
traffic now and then, and the goblins come more often. People stroll the sidewalk and
they are not targets: shooting one is -5 and a fright, nothing worse.

## How do you win?

Highest score. It is an endless drive.

## What should it look like?

A street at night: shops and towers, trees and street lights between them, people
walking, a far skyline and clouds drifting more slowly than the street (so it feels
deep). The car interior frames the view: pillars, roof, door sill, and the gun in the
bottom right corner with a flash and a tracer when it fires.

## Core loop

Watch the street roll by, spot the goblin, tap it before it is gone. Faster and faster.
