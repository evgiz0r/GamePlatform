# getaway

The player's sketch, in full: "small game like gta2". So: a city seen from straight
above, a car, the cops. Everything below is that, made small.

## What is it called?

getaway

## Who are you?

A small-time driver in a stolen car, in a neon city at night. Seen from above, the way
those old top-down crime games looked: buildings stick up out of the screen and lean away
from you as you drive past them, the streets have traffic and people on the pavements.

## What do you do?

Drive. Point where you want to go and the car turns and goes there. It slides when you
throw it round a corner, and it leaves skid marks.

A payphone is ringing somewhere (follow the arrow round your car). Drive into it to take
the job: a hot case to deliver across town before the clock runs out. Deliver it and you
get paid, plus a bonus for every second you had left. Too slow and the deal is off.

Keys: arrows / WASD steer (up means up the screen, not "forward"), space is the
handbrake. On a phone: put your thumb down anywhere and drag the way you want to go.

## What is trying to stop you?

The police. Every delivery adds a wanted star, and every star is one more police car
hunting you through the streets (up to five). They know the roads and they get faster.
If one touches you, you are busted. Getting busted knocks a star off. Three busts and
it is over.

Traffic gets in the way, and the parks are grass, so they slow you down. People on the
pavement jump out of your way.

## How do you win?

Highest score. Keep doing jobs for as long as you can stay out of handcuffs.

## What should it look like?

Night. Dark streets with yellow lane markings, street lamps pooling light, lit windows,
coloured neon on the rooftops, headlights, police lights flashing red and blue.

## Core loop

Answer the phone, race the case across town, and shake the cops, who grow in number with
every job you finish.
