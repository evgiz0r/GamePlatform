# showdown

> This is the design of ONE game, in the words of whoever is making it. The AI reads
> it before every change to this game and it outranks the AI's own taste. Bad
> handwriting is fine, so is changing your mind.

## What is it called?

showdown

## The ask

"Make 1v1 battler like battle royal style game"

## Who are you?

A fighter dropped into an arena with exactly one rival.

## What do you do?

Move and shoot. Your shots aim themselves at the rival — you choose where to stand and
when to pull the trigger.

## What is trying to stop you?

- **The rival.** It strafes, dodges, grabs loot and shoots back.
- **The storm.** Like a battle royale, a ring closes in. Outside it you lose hearts.
- Rocks block bullets, both ways.

## How do you win?

Last one standing wins the round. Then a tougher rival drops in. Survive as many rounds as
you can; your score is hits landed plus round wins.

## Loot

- red potion: one heart back
- blue potion: rapid fire for a few seconds

## Controls

- Keyboard: WASD / arrows to move, space (hold) to fire.
- Phone: drag on the left half to move, hold the right half to fire.
- Mouse: hold the button to fire.

## Core loop

> Rival shoots at you while the storm shrinks → dodge, grab loot, shoot back from cover →
> they drop → a tougher rival lands in a fresh storm.
