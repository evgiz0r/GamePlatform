# me

A 3D avatar of the person this kit belongs to, filmed doing things. Not a game: each level
is one short scene that plays itself, made to be recorded to a video.

What was asked (23 Nov 2026), in their words:

> what will it take to simulate my face a simulation ( separte to a city ).
> lets say i want to create a video where i do something ( explain, wave, walk, run, look )
> together with whatever happens in the scene

> go make a session responsible on creating me dancing, waving, walking, teaching on a board

## The scenes (levels)

1. **walk** -- down a pavement at night past street lamps, the camera alongside (10 s)
2. **wave** -- turns to the camera and waves, the camera easing in (7 s)
3. **dance** -- on a lit dance floor at 120 bpm: fists pumping in turn, then one finger to
   the ceiling (both moves from the photos he sent), the camera circling (12 s)
4. **teach** -- explains with his hands, turns and writes three lines on a whiteboard, then
   points at each line (17 s)

## The body

For now a stand-in: the kit's low-poly Quaternius person dressed like the photos (brick-red
V-neck tee, dark trousers, short brown hair). It moves like him but does not look like him.
The next step is a model with his real face (see "Later").

## Later (said, not built)

- His real face: a model made from daylight selfies (front with a neutral face, 45° left,
  45° right) or a short video turning his head, exported as .glb with a humanoid skeleton.
- Run, and "look" (looking around), as scenes.
- Putting him into a scene where other things happen around him (the city, for one).
