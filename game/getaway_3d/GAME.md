# getaway 3d

The 3D version of `getaway`. The player's words: "a 3d version. Bit bigger map, more
places to drive, more variance, pedestrians, street lights and more."

## What is it called?

getaway 3d

## Who are you?

A driver in a neon city at night, seen from high above and a little behind, so the
towers lean out at you as you pass. You start in a plain sedan; you can end up in a
taxi, an ambulance, a fire truck or a police car you stole.

## What do you do?

Drive. Point where you want to go and the car turns and goes there; it slides through
corners and leaves skid marks. Jobs pop up one after another, each against the clock:

- **a hot case**: answer the ringing payphone, deliver the case
- **a fare**: pick up the person waving at the kerb, drop them off
- **a hot car**: steal the parked car with the ring round it (you swap into it, and it
  drives its own way: vans are slow, the ambulance is quick and slippery), bring it to
  the garage

Get there with time to spare and you earn a bonus. Cash lies about on the streets too.
Street lamps can be knocked flat.

Keys: arrows / WASD steer (up is up the screen), space is the handbrake. On a phone: put
your thumb down anywhere and drag the way you want to go.

## What is trying to stop you?

The police. Every finished job adds a wanted star, and every star is another police car
hunting you (up to five), lights flashing on the buildings. They know the streets. A cop
on you fills the bust meter: get away before it closes. Busted knocks a star off; three
busts and it is over.

Traffic gets in the way and stops for people crossing. Parks are grass, so they are
slow. People jump out of the way, and get knocked over if they don't (they get up again).

## How do you win?

Highest score.

## What should it look like?

A bigger city: towers downtown, shops in the middle, little houses with trees round the
edge, parks with fountains, plazas, car parks, and some blocks joined together so the
streets are not all the same. Night: street lamps pooling light on the road, neon signs,
headlights, a minimap in the corner.

## Core loop

Take the job, race it across town against the clock, shake the cops who multiply with
every job you finish.
