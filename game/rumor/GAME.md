# rumor

> The player's design, in their words. This file outranks the AI's taste.

## What is it called?

rumor

## Who are you?

A kid in a school with many rooms and people. There is a rumor going around and I have
to find out what it is.

## What do you do?

Stroll around a 2D map and talk to people. Dialogs with multiple choice answers.

People don't tell me anything unless I answer some questions, or if I tell them who sent
me. Then I go through a mini RPG in the school, following the "main path", which can be
randomized.

## What is trying to stop you?

_(added while building, so the game has teeth)_

- The hall monitor patrols the hallway. Get caught and you lose a life.
- Every wrong answer costs a life. Three and the rumor becomes about you.
- The bell. When it rings, whatever you have not found out stays unknown.

## How do you win?

Follow the chain: each person sends you to the next one. The last one tells you the rumor.

## What should it look like?

A school. Rooms off a hallway, people in them, the animated characters from the kit.
Neon like the rest of the kit.

## Controls

Arrows or WASD to walk, or tap where you want to go (tap a person to walk up to them).
Walking into someone starts the talk. Space, J or a tap advances a line; up and down
pick an answer and space or a tap confirms it.

## Version one (what is built)

Four rooms off one hallway, four students, one hall monitor. The chain through the
students is shuffled every run. The first student gives you a lead for free. Each of the
others asks one question before they talk: either "who sent you?" or something the
previous student told you about them. The last one tells you the rumor.

Everything here is on purpose small. The point of this version is how the dialogs feel,
how walking around feels, and how talking to someone in the world works. Bigger maps,
branching dialogs, and people who remember you are for later.
