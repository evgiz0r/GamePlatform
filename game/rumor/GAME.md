# rumor

> The player's design, in their words. This file outranks the AI's taste.

## What is it called?

rumor

## Who are you?

Just me, pondering around a room full of people.

## What do you do?

Walk around. People ask me riddles and trivia. I get multiple answers to pick from.
If I get it wrong, that person does not talk about it anymore.

Questions like: biggest country, largest population, a hairy pet starting with C...

## What is trying to stop you?

Nothing chases you. The only thing at stake is the question itself: one shot per person,
and a wrong answer closes that door for good.

## How do you win?

Talk to everyone in the room. Every right answer is 100 points; all of them right is a
bonus on top.

## What should it look like?

One room, the animated characters from the kit wandering around it. Neon like the rest
of the kit.

## Controls

Made for a phone first. Tap anywhere to walk there. Hold and drag to steer. Tap a person
to walk up to them; walking into someone starts the talk. Tap the dialog to skip a line.
The answers are big buttons.

Keys still work: arrows or WASD to walk, space or J to advance, arrows to pick an answer.

## Version two (what is built)

The school, the chain of clues, the hall monitor and the bell from version one are gone.
They made the walking annoying and the marker that pointed you around the map was noise.

Now: one room, six people who wander slowly, each with one question drawn from a pool of
about thirty (trivia and classic riddles, four answers each, shuffled every run). Right or
wrong, that person is done; they have a short line for you if you come back. The round
ends when all six have asked. The line at the top counts how many have asked and how many
you got right.

The thing that was broken on a phone -- a dialog line with nothing to tap to move on --
is fixed: a tap anywhere advances, and the panel says so.
