# firefight

A first-person shooter where you only shoot. The player's words: "like drive by, but more
combat shooting style. The player moves on its own, and I just shoot as he watches around
and moves. I want to avoid using the controls to move -- just shoot, or aim, or zoom.
Not sure. Want to see how it goes." This is the first try at that.

## What is it called?

firefight

## Who are you?

Someone walking down a bending city street at night with a pistol, seen through their own
eyes. You never steer and you never stop: you keep walking, easing off only when someone is
right in front of you, and your eyes turn toward whoever is about to shoot.

(Second try. The first version walked from stop to stop and the goblins waited at
pre-placed spots; the player found that weak and the far-off street popping in annoying.
Now it flows, the goblins move, and fog plus the bends hide the end of the street.)

## What do you do?

Tap to shoot exactly where you tap. Six rounds, then a short reload on its own (tap the
gun in the corner to reload early). A shot to the head is worth more, so is dropping a
goblin while it runs. Keys and pad: arrows move the crosshair, A fires, B reloads.

## What is trying to stop you?

Goblins with guns, and they move:
- sprinting out of alleys and cross streets to dive behind parked cars and crates, popping
  up over them to shoot, and breaking for new cover further on;
- running straight down the road at you;
- rising over the edge of a roof;
- piling out of a van that comes down the other lane and screeches sideways in front of you.

A ring fills around a goblin while it aims (an arrow on the edge of the screen if it is
out of view); if it closes before you drop them, you are hit. Five lives. More of them
over time, and they aim faster. Now and then a person runs across the street: do not shoot
them, it costs a life.

## How do you win?

Highest score. An endless walk that keeps getting busier.

## What should it look like?

A neon street at night that bends left and right and fades into purple fog, buildings
on both sides, cross streets and alleys, parked cars, crates, street lights. A small
glowing marker over every goblin's head. Your gun in
the bottom right, kicking on every shot, with a muzzle flash.

## Not yet (ideas from the first sketch)

- Zoom: hold to zoom in for long shots.
- Aim that drifts with the walk, so steadying the shot is part of it.
- Different weapons.
