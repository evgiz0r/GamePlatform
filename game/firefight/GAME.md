# firefight

A first-person shooter where you only shoot. The player's words: "like drive by, but more
combat shooting style. The player moves on its own, and I just shoot as he watches around
and moves. I want to avoid using the controls to move -- just shoot, or aim, or zoom.
Not sure. Want to see how it goes." This is the first try at that.

## What is it called?

firefight

## Who are you?

Someone walking down a city street at night with a pistol, seen through their own eyes.
You never steer. You walk on your own to the next corner, stop, and look around at where
the trouble is; when it is dealt with you walk on.

## What do you do?

Tap to shoot exactly where you tap. Six rounds, then a short reload on its own (tap the
gun in the corner to reload early). A shot to the head is worth more. Keys and pad: arrows
move the crosshair, A fires, B reloads.

## What is trying to stop you?

Goblins with guns, hiding behind parked cars, crates and the edges of rooftops. They pop
out, take aim -- a ring fills up around them while they do -- and if the ring closes before
you drop them, they hit you. Five lives. Each stop has more of them, and they aim faster.
Now and then a person on the street pops up from cover too: do not shoot them, it costs a
life.

## How do you win?

Highest score. Clear a stop, walk on, the next one is harder. Endless.

## What should it look like?

A neon street at night, buildings on both sides, parked cars, street lights. Your gun in
the bottom right, kicking on every shot, with a muzzle flash.

## Not yet (ideas from the first sketch)

- Zoom: hold to zoom in for long shots.
- Aim that drifts with the walk, so steadying the shot is part of it.
- Different weapons.
